REINFORCE (policy gradient)

The arm takes actions, collects a trajectory, and updates the policy to make better actions more likely:

L = -E[G_t · log π(a_t|s_t)]

State (8D): joint angles (cos/sin), target position, fingertip error vector.

Reward: -dist(fingertip, target) — dense signal, no sparse reward problem.

Watch the training curve: early episodes are noisy. Around ep 200–400, the policy starts reliably reaching.
